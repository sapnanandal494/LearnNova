Daily Knowledge Booster: Neural Architecture

Question 1 of 3 stars +25 XP

Suppose an input image has dimension 32 × 32 × 3. It is convolved with 10 filters of size 5 × 5 with a stride of 1 and padding of 0. What is the spatial dimension of the resulting feature map before pooling?

calculate Derivation Formula: Output Feature Dimension

The formula is given by: O = ⌊(W − K + 2P) / S⌋ + 1.

  • Spatial width/height: ⌊(32 − 5 + 0) / 1⌋ + 1 = 27 + 1 = 28.
  • Channel depth matches the total number of filters: 10 filters = 10 output channels.
  • Therefore, the resulting tensor dimension is 28 × 28 × 10 (Option A).
